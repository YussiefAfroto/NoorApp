## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a peaceful, meditative, and contemporary Islamic mobile experience engineered specifically for Arabic Right-to-Left (RTL) ergonomics. The visual philosophy translates the concept of "Noor" (divine light) into an uncluttered, ethereal digital sanctum that feels weightless, spiritually uplifting, and deeply focused.
